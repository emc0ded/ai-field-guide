Appendix · Tiers

Tiers within each model

Most of these models ship in more than one size. Here’s what I ran into across Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT’s sub-tiers, and where each one actually hit a limit.

Model / Tier Speed Access Response Quality Hit a limit?
Claude Haiku Fast Free Fine for quick answers, basic coding, summaries. Smaller context. Not for multi-file or ambiguous work. Yes, on complex prompts
Claude Sonnet Balanced Free + Pro Strong for writing, coding, research. Available on free and paid plans. Yes, on intensive tasks
Claude Opus 4.6 Most capable Pro only Deep reasoning specialist. For advanced research and complex questions. Yes, burns limit fast
Gemini 3.1 Flash Fast EWU Google Quick, free, good reasoning all things considered. No
Gemini 3.1 Thinking Reasoning EWU Google Reasons step-by-step before answering. Slower, more accurate. No
Gemini 3.1 Pro Most capable EWU Google Highest tier reasoning. Good for complex research. Paid. No
ChatGPT Free Rate-limited Free General Q+A, writing, everyday requests. Very limited context. Yes
ChatGPT Copilot Full EWU Copilot Good for Microsoft 365 integrated tasks (email, Teams). No image generation. No
Rate limit note

Claude Opus burns through the limit faster than any other tier I used. Use it when you actually need depth, switch to Sonnet for general work.
