Model No. 05

Llama

Meta · Llama 4, free · Tested April 2026
Overview

Llama was compact and practical. It gave me what I asked for, and not much else. It also leaned hard on emoji in ways that didn’t really fit what I was asking about.

Llama is built for developers who want to self-host. The context window is big, the privacy posture is strong, and the running costs are under your control. In the browser, it feels like a model meant to be a building block, not a daily companion. The responses were shorter than the other five, and the least opinionated.

Developer
Meta
Access
Free, open-weights
Rating
3 / 5
Prompt Responses

Each prompt was delivered verbatim to every model. What follows are my notes on how Llama, specifically, answered.

Prompt 01 · Something I love

Physical media & the feeling of ritual

“Something about physical media feels more like an event than streaming ever does, like putting on a record, sliding in a disc, or cracking a book. I’m curious how much of that is the ritual itself and if I can manufacture that feeling.”

Llama took the question seriously and gave me practical ritual setups, appointment-style viewing, a one-tab rule, tying a format to a specific location. Less philosophical scaffolding than Claude or Grok. More prescriptive.

What I noticed

Llama didn’t linger. It got to the tactics fast. Brief, but usable.

Prompt 02 · Something new

A beginner’s garden in the Pacific Northwest

“I am a beginner to gardening and live in the Pacific Northwest. What are the easiest vegetables or herbs to start with, and what do I actually need to know in my first season?”

Llama used tables for what to plant when, and kept the explanatory copy short. Easy to scan. The warmth that ChatGPT brings to a beginner answer wasn’t there.

As a teacher

The tone wasn’t what I was hoping for. Llama leans on emojis in places where they don’t fit. Practical, but not particularly warm.

Prompt 03 · Design challenge

Walk me through your creative process

“I need help designing my portfolio website to showcase my web design projects, apps and games, and social media management work. Walk me through your creative process.”

Short response. Llama framed the answer around “structure before style” and listed the sections I’d need. It didn’t go deep into tactics the way ChatGPT or Claude did.

Creative problem solving

All-in approach. No questions. If you hand Llama a brief, it gives you back a brief-shaped answer and stops.

Prompt 04 · Design concept

Explain branding strategy

“Explain branding strategy development in a way that makes sense to me.”

Llama reduced branding to one line: decide what you want to be famous for in one sentence, then build a system that proves it. Direct. Sparse. Useful if you already have context.

Concrete vs abstract

The definition was tight. The supporting examples just weren’t there. Next to Claude’s layered answer, Llama’s felt like a summary.

Prompt 05 · Real design problem

Modernize a salon, spa, and barbershop brand

“Here’s a design problem I’m facing: modernizing a salon, spa, and barbershop brand (colors, fonts) and website. Help me solve it.”

Llama gave me three directions, Modern Nature Spa, Urban Classic, Minimal Luxe Hybrid, with palettes, typography, and a strategy-vs- tactics table that made the tradeoffs easy to see. No follow-up questions.

UI mockup generated

No mockup. Three brand directions with design specs, clearly laid out. Without a visual reference, I had to imagine the output.

Assessment
Strengths
  • Big context window handles long projects well.
  • Privacy-friendly if self-hosted.
  • Table-based structure is easy to scan.
  • Answers get to the point.
Limitations
  • You need real technical chops to self-host.
  • Tone slips into emoji overload.
  • Doesn’t ask anything before answering.
  • Weaker on creative work than ChatGPT or Claude.
Final Rating
3out of 5

I didn’t love the tone, and what I actually need doesn’t overlap with what Llama is really good at, self-hosting, privacy-sensitive deployments, developer workflows. In a different role, this would be the right pick.
