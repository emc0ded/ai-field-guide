Model No. 01

ChatGPT

OpenAI · Plus plan · Tested April 2026
Overview

ChatGPT was the strongest brainstorming partner of the six. It was also the one that tried hardest to sound like a friend — which helped when I was exploring, and grated when I already knew what I was doing.

Every response came back structured, bulleted, and easy to skim. ChatGPT leaned on images whenever it could, which I appreciated. But when I asked it about areas where I already had expertise, it kept defaulting to a beginner-friendly register. It was cute and competent, and it stayed at the surface.

Developer
OpenAI
Access
Plus (paid)
Rating
4 / 5
Prompt Responses

Each prompt was delivered verbatim to every model. What follows are my notes on how ChatGPT, specifically, answered.

Prompt 01 · Something I love

Physical media & the feeling of ritual

“Something about physical media feels more like an event than streaming ever does — like putting on a record, sliding in a disc, or cracking a book. I’m curious how much of that is the ritual itself and if I can manufacture that feeling.”

ChatGPT framed it as a psychology question: ritual, delayed gratification, multi-sensory engagement, commitment. It didn’t wave the feeling away as nostalgia. It gave me concrete tactics — a “start ritual,” intentional friction, visual anchors — and at the end offered to design a whole “media ritual system.”

What I noticed

It tried to relate to me before answering: “You’re picking up on something very real…” The framing was warm. Some of the points felt recycled from previous answers, but the reasoning was sound.

Prompt 02 · Something new

A beginner’s garden in the Pacific Northwest

“I am a beginner to gardening and live in the Pacific Northwest. What are the easiest vegetables or herbs to start with, and what do I actually need to know in my first season?”

ChatGPT broke the answer into vegetable categories with emoji, added a “what you actually need to know” section with soil and watering rules, and ended on a mindset shift: this is experimenting, not keeping things alive perfectly.

As a teacher

I loved that it included images. Because it knows me, it also knew I care about how things look. The bullet layout was easy to scan.

Where I got skeptical

It said carrots are “plant and forget.” I’ve heard they’re actually finicky. ChatGPT is skimmable, but it doesn’t go deep.

Prompt 03 · Design challenge

Walk me through your creative process

“I need help designing my portfolio website to showcase my web design projects, apps and games, and social media management work. Walk me through your creative process.”

It handed me an 11-step workflow — story, content strategy, wireframing, visual direction, interaction design, case studies, layout ideas specific to my vibe, technical stack, what makes my portfolio special, its actual workflow order, and a “next level” idea referencing my existing scrollytelling project.

Creative problem solving

Jumped straight to solutions. Didn’t ask clarifying questions up front. But the answer was specific — it knew what I’d already made and built on it.

Prompt 04 · Design concept

Explain branding strategy

“Explain branding strategy development in a way that makes sense to me.”

ChatGPT framed branding as “designing a personality + perception system” and walked through seven layers: core, audience, positioning, personality, visuals, messaging, experience. It anchored every layer to my salon project specifically — not abstract theory.

Concrete vs abstract

Heavy on examples, light on theory. That worked for me. It gave me sentence-level positioning statements I could actually use.

Prompt 05 · Real design problem

Modernize a salon, spa, and barbershop brand

“Here’s a design problem I’m facing: modernizing a salon, spa, and barbershop brand (colors, fonts) and website. Help me solve it.”

This was the longest answer of the five. It delivered a complete brand evolution: a layered palette with exact hex codes, a serif-plus-sans font system, a homepage structure, and a text-form wireframe for the hero section. It called the overall direction “Modern ritual beauty house.”

UI mockup generated

Yes — an ASCII-drawn wireframe of the hero, plus full palette and typography pairings. Not interactive, but usable as a direction.

Assessment
Strengths
  • Images and visual examples land well.
  • Skimmable bullet structure across all answers.
  • Anchors abstract ideas to my actual projects.
  • Offers concrete next steps and code recommendations.
Limitations
  • Stays beginner-friendly even when I signal expertise.
  • Bullet-point pattern repeats; answers start to feel recycled.
  • Occasional factual softness (the carrots example).
  • No citations, so claims have to be taken on faith.
Final Rating
4out of 5

Really strong for all sorts of tasks. I reached for it more than any other model during testing. I just don’t find it as conversational as Claude.
